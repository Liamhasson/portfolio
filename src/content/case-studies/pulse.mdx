<MiddleBlock kind="problem" title="Problem">

Users quit before the habit.

Fitness apps lose people before a habit forms. In a study of 389,481 Fitbod users, training consistently in the first 28 days was the strongest predictor of sticking with it ([Conti et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.3389/fspor.2026.1855668)). BEAT81 and Urban Sports Club sell access to classes. Pulse is built around the habit.

I designed Pulse around four milestones, each with its own job:

- **Day 7:** the first habit loop.
- **Day 30:** a routine. The first month predicts who stays.
- **Day 60:** a habit. Habits take a median of 66 days to become automatic ([Lally et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674)).
- **Day 90:** staying power.

</MiddleBlock>

<MiddleBlock kind="signature" title="Solution">

Goal-based onboarding.

Twelve screens. Screens 1–3 frame the app and learn your goal; 4–9 walk you through your first booking; 10–12 give the tour, ask for permissions and close.

</MiddleBlock>

<MiddleBlock kind="supporting" title="How it works">

Challenges without pressure.

Overview, reflect, progress: a Training Log for how sessions felt, and Challenges that are private, progress-based and unlimited. The brand carries the same promise: you set the goal, the app shows the way.

</MiddleBlock>
