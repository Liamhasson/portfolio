<MiddleBlock kind="problem" title="Problem">

A linear site vs an investor meeting.

Cyvore was raising its first round. Its website was linear: about 35 seconds of non-stop scrolling to reach a section, and no story holding it together. Fine for a visitor, wrong for a founder presenting live, who needs any answer in one click.

Nobody had asked for a redesign. I pitched one.

</MiddleBlock>

<MiddleBlock kind="signature" title="Solution">

One screen, everything.

I rebuilt the information architecture around the meeting, not the scroll. The main screen swaps between Security, Exposure, Coverage and Enables with hover and click animations, while both navigation bars stay visible the whole time. Wherever the presentation is, every section is one move away.

The replica below is refined for this portfolio: the content and layout are as shipped; type, colour and depth are tightened.

</MiddleBlock>

<MiddleBlock kind="supporting" title="How it works">

Motion with a job.

Every animation carries the founder from one answer to the next. A 7-second sequence tells the product's mission: a phishing link lands in a video call, and Cyvore blocks it. The design system behind it: type, colour tokens and component states.

</MiddleBlock>
