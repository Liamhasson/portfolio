Doubled

A part of the subject repeats, offset and a little larger, so the frame looks like it glitched. Always from one moment, so it reads as an error, not a collage.

CS 03 · Problem · 1 screenDRAFT

Problem

Loud is easy. Being remembered isn't. DRAFT

Marni Came was a new band in a crowded Berlin scene: no label, no budget, and a lot of polished competition. My BA research looked at how four artists built a persona (Bowie, Cobain, Grohl and Måneskin) and asked what a band our size could bring that others didn't. DRAFT

CS 09 · Challenges · 1.2 screens · the crowd's proof, with the Lido fan photoDRAFT

Challenges

01

Choosing the first single DRAFT

What happened. Three finished songs, money for one music video.
What I did. Posted a snippet of each and let our followers choose.
What it changed. The debut single was theirs: 176 likes and 37 comments, the band's most-engaged post at the time.

02

Filming a show with no crew DRAFT

What happened. We wanted a live video and had no camera team.
What I did. Brought thirteen cameras to the show and handed a couple to friends in the crowd, then edited it.
What it changed. The Dopamine live video, filmed partly by the audience.

Impact
