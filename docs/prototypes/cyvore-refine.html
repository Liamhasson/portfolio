Cyvore refinement — compare before choosing

One working copy of the replica (hover or click columns; the lower bar switches chapters). Flip between A, B and C with the switch or the keys 1, 2, 3. All three keep Cyvore's violet-black, Michroma and the purple open column; B and C only refine around them. Sources: Evervault, Twingate and Doppler on styles.refero.design.

Version
Chapter

A · As shipped

Cyvore's site, as on the case-study board

  • Michroma for everything, including 13px descriptions.
  • Pure white text.
  • Depth from purple glows: around the site, the stage, the active tab, both CTAs.
  • Radii 20 / 18 / 14 / 12 as built.

B · Evervault only

Colour and text tokens, type roles

  • Michroma stays for headings, nav, buttons and column names; descriptions, sub-heads and captions move to Inter 15px.
  • Cool off-white text (#dfe1f4, brightest #ececfb), quieter secondary text (#babcd2).
  • Shadows tinted violet (#2e2a4c), not black.
  • Components, radii and glows unchanged.

C · Evervault + Twingate

B, plus Twingate's components and Doppler's rules

  • Everything in B.
  • One light source (behind the site); the stage, tabs and cards get hairline insets instead of glows.
  • Engineered radii: 16 frame, 12 surfaces, 10 cards, 8 tabs; pills stay pills.
  • Closed columns step up from the canvas (#161025) with quieter names.
  • One glowing CTA per screen: the hero's; the nav's goes outline.
  • Open column: a directional violet gradient with a top highlight, instead of a radial glow.
