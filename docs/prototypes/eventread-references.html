Lusion: project page system

https://lusion.co/projects
  • The page scrolls sideways. The mouse wheel moves a horizontal strip. The first panel is text only: title, two short paragraphs, Services and Links lists, and a "Launch project" pill. Every panel after it is media in rounded frames. The frames are about 637px tall in a 900px viewport (y 145 to 782), with a gap of about 80px between them.
  • The project brings its accent colour. The Lusion chrome (logo, "scroll to explore", section labels, inline links) takes each project's colour. Oryzo uses #DC5000 with cream text #FFEDD7. Devin uses teal #64E1CE. Atlas uses pink #F65893 with #FFFDE0. The page backgrounds are near-black and tinted per project: ≈#191410, ≈#121413, ≈#181818.
  • Media sits inside the strip. Frames hold screenshots of the product site, video loops and 3D renders. Some frames have their own full-bleed backgrounds, like the light-blue Devin panel. Text never sits on top of the frames.
  • The end shows the next project. The strip stops with a light panel (≈#F3F4F9) peeking in from the right, about 360px wide. It holds the next title at 72px in grey rgb(194,195,199) and the label "NEXT PROJECT ——→" at 14px. Clicking it did not navigate in headless Chromium, so the transition between projects was not observed.

Lusion: Oryzo AI

https://lusion.co/projects/oryzo_ai
  • Opening: the text panel sits on the left, about 330px wide. The first media frame is already bleeding in from the right in the first viewport, so the work is visible before any scrolling.
  • Rhythm: big product-site frames (hero, "sustainability" type moment) alternate with pairs of smaller frames (render plus texture). A founder video frame comes late in the strip.
  • End: the last frame is a warm "sketch" image. The next-project panel ("Atlas Motion") cuts in on the right.

Lusion: Devin AI (closest to a SaaS product story)

https://lusion.co/projects/devin_ai
  • Product UI is the hero. Website screenshots in a light bezel frame start in the first viewport. Real UI states follow: a customer-story page, a workspace menu, an integrations grid.
  • Light panels inside a dark page. The page switches to a pale blue-grey full-height panel (≈#D2DDE7) for a sequence of UI cards. One centre card is in focus and the cards on either side are angled in 3D. It is captioned "Engaging visuals" in small white type.
  • Media ends on a device shot: a laptop on a dark set shows the product, followed by the next-project panel ("Of The Oak").

Lusion: Atlas Motion

https://lusion.co/projects/atlas_motion
  • Opening: the first frame is a line-drawn "grid" placeholder that resolves into the product site. The scroll reveals a wipe from one frame to the next, and a vertical seam between two frames is visible mid-transition.
  • Type moments come from the product site ("INNOVATE", "THE ATLAS ECOSYSTEM"). Lusion's own words stay in the intro panel only.
  • 36 wheel steps of 600px did not reach the end of this strip. The last capture is still media.

Clay: Interos (light + navy, SaaS risk platform)

https://clay.global/work/interos
  • Type scale: UniversalSans. Title h1 is 74/81.4px, weight 740, tracking −2.96px (−0.04em). Section h2 is 52/57.2px, 740, −1.56px (−0.03em). The intro line is 28/39.2px, 500. Body is 22/29.7px, 500, −0.44px, in columns about 516–637px wide. Ink is #050411.
  • Rhythm: sections are 1,100–1,600px tall with 80px top and bottom padding. Background bands step from white to a lavender tint #E9ECFD, then to three full navy sections #031046.
  • How product media sits: tablet and phone frames sit on the navy band, with the tablet cropped by the band edges. There are side-by-side "Before / After" panels with 20px captions underneath.
  • End: "Next Case Study" at 28px with an arrow on white. The next title (PalmPalm, 74px) is in the DOM but had not drawn in the capture. A dark "Let's Talk" footer follows.

Ramotion: Okta (light + navy, identity SaaS)

https://www.ramotion.com/okta/
  • Type scale: Graphik. Title h1 is 92/101.2px, weight 600, −3.2px. Section h2 is 36/43.2px, 600. Body is 18/27px, 400, in columns about 532–640px wide. The testimonial quote is 24/37.2px. Ink is #262626 on a page background of #FAFAFA.
  • Navy is the brand colour, not the page: navy ≈#012F84 appears in headings (a two-tone "Iconography & Illustrations Hierarchy" with the second part in grey), icon tiles, and one full navy tile per grid.
  • How product media sits: real product screens (docs, help centre, sign-in) are arranged as a flat collage of cards on a cool grey #F0F1F5, with no device frames. The hero crops a laptop at the top edge.
  • End: client quote with portrait, then "Let's talk" and the footer. No next-project link.

Clay: Tipalti (light, finance SaaS, product UI)

https://clay.global/work/tipalti
  • Same Clay type scale as Interos: 74px title, 52px h2 (for example "Bringing a Human Touch to Tech", set on two lines), 22px body, ink #06070A. Brand yellow is used only in the hero band and one awards card.
  • How product media sits: UI fragments (cards, a chart, a newsletter form, an "AI" chip) float on white as separate components, not as a full-page screenshot. Mobile screens are shown in a row on black.
  • End: a white band reads "Next Case Study", then "STC Bank →" at 74px with an underline. Directly below, a full-width image of the next project shows a "View case study" cursor pill.
