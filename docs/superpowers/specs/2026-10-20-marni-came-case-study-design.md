## 1. Intent

Marni Came was Liam's Berlin rock band (active until July 2024). Liam was the band's brand and creative lead: frontman,
songwriter, designer, director and producer. The case study shows the band's identity **as one finished brand at its
best**: the 2024 identity with refined visuals. It never presents itself as a redesign or a before and after.

Why it exists:
- **Another side of Liam.** Nordic Logic shows precision; Marni Came shows a loud, raw art director with a real
  strategy underneath.
- **Proof of a brand system.** It shows a system (logo, colour, type, four image moves, rules) carried across
  video, photography, posters, release artwork, merch and a website.

**Who reads it:** the same in-house hiring manager as the other studies (frame spec §1). They skim for two to three
minutes, looking for what Liam did, how he thinks, what happened, and whether the evidence is honest.

### Goals
- A brand case study as strong as the two Behance references Liam likes, in flow and imagery:
  - Adriana Fernández's "PORTFOLIO_2025": collage, real photos taped down, a zine feel, imagery telling the story;
  - Manu Pagliosa Arch: a manifesto, symbols with meaning, a named type group and palette, applications on real
    objects.
- It reads as part of the portfolio: same frame, same facts in the same places.
- A new band website, built and playable, shown inside the study.

### Non-goals
- Recreating the old marnicame.com.
- Showing social media posts (Liam doesn't stand behind them).
- A store, ticketing or any working commerce.
- Changing the About page.

### Anti-goals: a failure even if it works
- It looks like a redesign story ("before / after", "I fixed the inconsistency").
- It reads as a template restyled dark: the middle must feel like a brand book, not Problem/Solution boxes.
- Anything glows that the camera didn't catch (§4.4).
- Fast strobing on the page.
- Any invented fact: show times, prices, addresses, quotes or numbers that aren't in the band's own material.

## 3. The study, block by block

Copy is drafted on the board for Liam's line-by-line approval, as for the other studies. Section titles are the plain
recruiter titles (frame spec §3.7); the creative phrase opens the section's text.

### Hero (shared block, Marni Came content)
- **Title:** Marni Came · Brand identity
- **Line:** to be approved; draft: "A Berlin rock band that kept its mistakes, in sound and in picture."
- **Media:** the regraded Dopamine-live-at-Badehaus loop, full-bleed (`hero-badehaus.mp4`, 8.6 s), with the distressed
  wordmark over it.
  - It is the largest hero of the five, since this study has no device frame.
  - The layout keeps the shared hero's title and line positions, so a skimmer finds them in the usual place.
- **Link:** none (the band is inactive). The website (§3, Website) is reached from inside the page.

### At a glance
- **Role:** Brand identity, art direction, video & web design.
- **Timeline:** 2022 – 2024.
- **Team:** Band of four; Imri Agmon (co-director, Badehaus).
- **Tools:** to be confirmed by Liam; known so far: Photoshop, Premiere Pro, Pro Tools, Ableton, Figma, Next.js.
- **Type:** Band project, alongside a BA at BIMM Berlin.

### Problem
- **Opens:** a short first line, drafted on the board.
- **Content:** from the BA research.
  - A new band in a crowded Berlin scene, with no budget, competing for attention with polished, overproduced acts.
  - The BA's four positioning case studies (Bowie, Cobain, Grohl, Måneskin) are named in one line, not shown.
  - The audience profile is one line.
- **Visual:** one full-bleed archive image in the brand's grade (the Lido crowd, or a frame from the End of Term gig).

### Solution (signature, 2 screens)
- **Screen 1, the idea:** "Broken on purpose." with the cycling word (board spread 02), and the two halves:
  - **The sound:** not overproduced; the cracked note stays in.
  - **The crowd:** the audience is part of the band, not its customer.
- **Screen 2, the effects switch:** one large archive photo (a still from the After Heartbreaks video, the half-lit
  face, or the Lido stage) with four toggles: Slice, Burn, Doubled, Lens.
  - Each toggle applies that move live; any combination works.
  - Under each toggle sits its one-line rule from the board.
  - Default state: all four off, so the first click shows the brand being made.
  - **Reduced motion:** the toggles still work, but each switch is instant, with no animated transition.
  - **Phones:** the same, with the four toggles in a row under the image.
  - The four moves are built once and shared with the website (§5), so the switch and the site are literally the same
    system.

### Identity (supporting)
- Logo, colour and type, as board spreads 03–06, in one sequence:
  - **Logo:** the three versions, plus the clean master shown at small size.
  - **Colour:** in proportion, with the Burn row.
  - **Type:** the specimen (no words), then the sans at work in the band's own pieces (credit block, Badehaus ticket,
    singles tracklist).

### Imagery (supporting)
- The four moves (spread 07): one image each, with captions as approved.
- **Doubled:** the rule and the moodboard (spread 09), made entirely of the video's own doubles.
- **The glow line** is folded into the moves: one caption under Burn with the allowed/banned pair as two small images.

### Applications (supporting)
- **Release artwork:** the After Heartbreaks cover (route A).
- **Show posters:** the four.
- **The mockups Liam shoots** (the shot list itself stays off the page):
  - 12" sleeve, wheatpaste, stage flag, crowd shirt, ticket, press kit, the site on a phone;
  - each lands where its board placeholder sits;
  - until a shot exists, its slot is left out of the page, never faked.
- **Composition:** the Adriana-style collage applies here. Real prints, posters and the ticket are photographed as
  objects and laid in a loose grid with tape and overlaps, but the overlaps never cover a face or a title. This is the
  one zine moment of the study.

### Website (supporting, interactive)
- **What it shows:** the release-week site (§5), embedded in the page as a live frame the reader can scroll and click,
  at a desktop size scaled to the column, with an "Open the site" link to its full-screen route.
- **On phones:** the frame is replaced by three still screens and the link.

### Challenges (shared block; happened → did → changed)
These carry the crowd half of the idea (Decision 18). The Lido fan photo sits beside them. Copy is approved on the
board; every fact comes from Liam or the BA.
1. **Choosing the first single.**
   - What happened: three songs, one video budget.
   - What I did: put the choice to the followers.
   - What it changed: the debut single was theirs, and the post drew 176 likes and 37 comments, the band's most-engaged.
2. **Filming a show with no crew.**
   - What happened: no budget for a camera team.
   - What I did: brought thirteen cameras and handed some to friends in the crowd, then edited and graded it.
   - What it changed: the Dopamine live video, shot by the audience (BA p. "thirteen cameras").
3. **Optional third:** only if Liam gives one that isn't about the crowd (for example the mix brief: keep the mistakes,
   lose the noise). Otherwise two items, which the frame allows.

### Impact (shared block)
- **Only what happened** (frame spec §3.2), from the BA, re-checked against the PDF at build time:
  - reach up 74.5% to 4,913 accounts;
  - 386 engaged;
  - 1,043 followers;
  - the venues played (Lido, Badehaus twice, the Emergenza semi-finals), without dates;
  - three singles released.
- No streaming numbers unless Liam supplies them.

## 4. Visual world

### 4.1 Typography
- **Liam's own words** follow the frame rule (§3.4): section titles, opening lines, body and labels are set in Geist and
  Geist Mono, as on every study.
- **Brand material** carries the brand's fonts: Cormorant Garamond and Archivo inside the spreads, plates, posters,
  covers and the website.
- This matches how Nordic Logic's Brand section works: the page's voice is Liam's, and the brand appears inside it.
- *My call:* the board set its explanatory copy in the brand fonts for presentation. The page moves that copy to Geist
  so the five studies read as one portfolio.

### 4.2 Colour and ground
- The page ground is Blackout; text is Bone.
- Title red appears only in brand material, never in Liam's titles. The shared section titles stay Bone.
- A film-grain overlay sits on the whole page (the board's), static under reduced motion.

### 4.3 Images
- Every image is real archive material, treated with the moves.
- The treatment pipeline is the board's script (scratchpad `mc/treat.py`), moved into the repo as a build-time tool
  under `tools/marni-came/` so every asset can be regenerated at 2×.
- **Burn layers** are pure light taken from no-subject frames of the video (e.g. 62.4 s), never footage carrying
  another moment.

### 4.4 Light and motion
- **Glow:** follows Decision 9.
- **Flashes:** no more than 3 a second. The idea line swaps hard every 1.6 s.
- **Reduced motion:** loops become posters, the cycling word stops on "Broken", and Doubled and Burn animations
  become their still frames.

### 4.5 Sound
- One control in the study's shell: "Sound off / on". Off by default; it starts only on a click.
- **Plays:** the After Heartbreaks master (AAC 128 kbps, about 3.4 MB, loaded on first click).
- It mutes when the tab is hidden, and it stops on leaving the page. Its state doesn't persist across visits.
- **One audio state for the page and the embedded site** (*my call*). The study's shell owns playback; the site inside
  the iframe asks the shell to play a track (`postMessage`) instead of playing on its own, so two masters never play at
  once and one toggle reflects both. Opened full screen, the site plays on its own.
- The hero's Badehaus loop is always muted; the sound control plays the After Heartbreaks master, the release the page
  is built around (*my call*).

## 5. The band website (release week)

A real, built site. It is designed as Marni Came's own site in the week After Heartbreaks came out.

**Where it lives:** a route in this repo, `/marni-came/site`, with its own layout (no portfolio chrome, `noindex`).
- The case study embeds it in an iframe and links to it.
- *My call*: one repo, one deploy, and the reader stays on liamhasson.com.

**Pages** (one scrolling page with anchored sections, plus the nav):

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Home | The After Heartbreaks video loop (`web-ah-loop.mp4`, 6 s; a longer cut may be graded from the master video) behind "NEW SINGLE · OUT NOW" and "AFTER HEARTBREAKS" in one serif line. Buttons: Watch the video (opens the full video in a lightbox), Listen (starts the master through the sound control). |
| Music | The three singles as a numbered list (the board's tracklist style); each plays its master with a waveform (the playground's `peaks.json` approach). |
| Videos | After Heartbreaks and Dopamine live at Badehaus: poster frames that open the full videos. |
| Shows | The venues as a list (venue · city), with the four posters; no dates outside the posters (Decision 20). No ticket links. |
| Merch | The crowd shirt and the stage flag as designed pieces, shown from Liam's mockup shots; no prices, no cart. |
| Band | The four members with one photo each from the photoshoot, names and instruments. No contact details. |

**Motion on the site:**
- **Slice:** a WebGL pass on section images on scroll; bands shift by a few pixels, then settle.
- **Burn:** a light layer breathing in from one edge on the hero and posters.
- **Doubled:** on hover, a masked part of the image is screened back larger and offset, as in the video.
- All within §4.4: no more than 3 flashes a second, and still under reduced motion.

**Nav:** the distressed wordmark (from the SVG), with Music · Videos · Shows · Merch · Band, and the sound toggle at
the right. On phones, a full-screen menu.
